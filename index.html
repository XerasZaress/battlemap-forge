Water & lava

Exports are still images, so the current shows as caustics, streaks and cracked crust rather than motion. The preview animates so you can aim the flow. Lava creeps at a fraction of the water speed, the way molten rock should.

Turn baked lighting off if your VTT does its own dynamic lighting — the Universal VTT export carries the light sources either way.

Trace an existing map ✕

Load any map image (Czepeku, Patreon art, a scan) and paint walls over it, then export a Universal VTT with line-of-sight built in.

Adjust until the on-screen grid lines up with the art — this matters most, because everything below keys off it.

Automatic tracing

Works best on maps with dark walls around lit floors. Raise sensitivity to catch faint walls (and more noise), lower it to keep only strong ones. Then fix up by hand with the Partition tool and cut doorways with Door.

Tool options ✕

Partitions sit on the lines between squares, so a wall never eats a 5 ft square. With the Door tool, click a partition to cut a doorway — click again for a secret door.

Prefab rooms ✕

Pick a room, then click the map to stamp it. Rooms placed side by side share the wall between them.

Make your own

Drag a rectangle on the map. Its terrain, partitions and furniture become a reusable prefab.

Room

Drag the room on the map to move it. Lifting a room restores whatever it was covering.
